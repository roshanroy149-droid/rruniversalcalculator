TallyBench / About
// ABOUT

Numbers you can trust, answered fast.

TallyBench is a personal finance resource at its core — loans, mortgages, taxes, investing, and debt payoff — built around one idea: get a straight answer without a sign-up, an account, or a data trail. It also covers health, school, and everyday math, because good tools shouldn't need ten different apps.

What this site is

TallyBench started as a single percentage calculator and grew into a finance site first — loans, mortgages, refinancing, car loans, rent-vs-buy, tax, paychecks, retirement, net worth, savings goals, and debt payoff — that also picked up a full workbench of health tools (BMI, calorie/BMR, macros, pregnancy and ovulation calculators), education tools (GPA, a scientific calculator, and more), and everyday math along the way. Every tool runs entirely in your browser — nothing you type is sent anywhere or stored.

How accurate is this?

Every formula on this site is built to match standard, publicly documented methods — the same amortization formula banks use for loans, the Mifflin-St Jeor equation for calorie needs, official tax bracket structures for income tax. Where a calculator involves judgment calls or simplifications (like the tax calculator's country-level estimates), that's stated directly on the page, not buried in fine print.

Who runs it

TallyBench is built, run, and maintained by Venros Tech. You can reach us at helpdesktallybench@gmail.com — that address goes to the people who actually build the tools, not a support queue.

It's an independent project. It isn't affiliated with any bank, government tax authority, or financial institution — the calculators are general tools for estimates and everyday math, not professional financial, tax, medical, or legal advice. The full terms are on the terms page.

How this site is funded

TallyBench is free to use and supported by display advertising (Google AdSense). No calculator result, personal detail, or usage history is ever sold — see the Privacy Policy for the full detail on how advertising cookies work here.

Contact

Spotted a bug, an incorrect formula, or want to suggest a calculator that's missing? Reach out at helpdesktallybench@gmail.com.
