The Advance Tax Cliff: Why ₹100 Short on 15 September Costs ₹1,083

At 1% a month, deferring advance tax costs a reasonable 12% a year. But s.234C attaches a 36% trigger to the September instalment, and missing it by ₹100 costs ₹1,083 on a ₹4 lakh liability.

About TallyBench

What this is, and how the numbers work.

TallyBench is a finance-first calculator site — loans, mortgages, taxes, investing — that also covers health, school, and everyday math, one question at a time. No account, no app download, no data stored anywhere but your own browser tab.

How is tip calculated?

Multiply the bill by the tip percentage, then divide the total by the number of people. TallyBench does this live as you type.

Is the BMI number exact?

BMI is a screening estimate based on height and weight only — it doesn't account for muscle mass, age, or frame size, so treat the category as a starting point, not a diagnosis.

How is the loan payment formula calculated?

TallyBench uses the standard fixed-rate amortization formula, converting your annual rate to a monthly rate and solving for a level payment across the full term.

Is my password generator output stored anywhere?

No. Passwords are generated using your browser's built-in random number generator and never leave the page.
