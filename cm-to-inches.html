Quick reference

Common centimeter-to-inch conversions.

CentimetersInches

How do I convert cm to inches?

Divide the centimeter value by 2.54: inches = cm ÷ 2.54. 1 inch is defined as exactly 2.54 cm by international agreement, so this conversion is exact, not an approximation.

How do I convert inches to cm?

Multiply the inch value by 2.54: cm = inches × 2.54. For example, 10 inches × 2.54 = 25.4 cm.

Where does 2.54 come from?

The international inch was formally standardized as exactly 2.54 cm in 1959 by agreement between the US, UK, and other Commonwealth countries, replacing several slightly different national definitions that existed before then.

How do I convert cm to feet and inches?

Divide by 2.54 to get total inches, then split that number into feet (divide by 12) and remaining inches. A 183 cm height, for instance, is 183 ÷ 2.54 = 72.05 inches, and 72.05 ÷ 12 = exactly 6 feet with a sliver left over — so 183 cm reads as "6 foot" on a US height chart. This two-step split is the most common reason people search cm-to-inch conversions in the first place: height, not just raw measurements.

Where does this conversion actually come up?

Beyond height, it shows up constantly in everyday shopping and DIY: TV and monitor sizes are advertised in inches (a "55-inch" screen measures 139.7 cm diagonally) everywhere except a handful of metric-only markets; sewing and woodworking patterns often mix cm and inch measurements depending on where they were published; and international shipping or furniture listings frequently quote dimensions in whichever unit the manufacturer's home country uses, leaving the buyer to convert.

Is there a quick way to estimate cm to inches in my head?

Divide the centimeter figure by 2.5, then shave off about 2%. For 175 cm: 175 ÷ 2.5 = 70, minus 2% ≈ 68.6 — close to the exact 68.9 inches, off by less than half an inch for most everyday heights and lengths. It's a handy sanity check when you don't have a calculator handy, but use the exact ÷ 2.54 figure above whenever precision matters.

Worked example: a 175 cm height converts to 175 ÷ 2.54 ≈ 68.9 inches (5 ft 8.9 in), and a 183 cm height converts to almost exactly 6 ft 0 in.
