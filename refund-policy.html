Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: July 2026. This covers purchases of the TallyStock desktop application. The calculators and articles on this site are free, so nothing here applies to them.

The short version: if TallyStock won't install or activate on your computer and we can't fix it for you, you get your money back within 7 days of purchase. Because it's a downloadable product with no trial, we'd rather refund you than have you stuck with software you can't use.

Cancellation

TallyStock is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. There is no recurring billing, so there is nothing to cancel and no way to be charged again automatically. Your licence simply continues to work.

A purchase can't be cancelled once the licence key has been issued and delivered, since delivery is immediate — but it may qualify for a refund under the section below.

When we will refund you

We'll issue a full refund if you request it within 7 days of purchase and any of the following apply:

  • The software won't install or run on your computer, and we can't resolve it with you.
  • Your licence key doesn't work or never arrived, and we can't deliver a working one.
  • You were charged more than once for the same purchase, or charged in error.
  • The software doesn't do something the product page said it does. If we described it inaccurately, that's on us.

If you bought the wrong edition — Standard when you needed Pro, or the reverse — contact us. We'd normally sort that out by swapping your key or charging/refunding the difference rather than making you buy twice.

When we may not refund you

Being straight with you about this up front, because the product is downloadable and delivered instantly:

  • More than 7 days have passed since purchase.
  • The software works as described and you've simply changed your mind or no longer need it.
  • You want a refund because of a feature TallyStock never claimed to have. The product page lists what's included; please check it before buying.
  • Data loss where backups weren't kept. TallyStock stores data only on your computer and includes a backup feature; we have no access to your data and cannot recover it.
  • The licence key has been shared, resold, or published, or the software has been tampered with.

None of this limits rights you have under Indian consumer-protection law that can't be excluded by a policy.

Before you ask for a refund — please try support first

Most install and activation problems are fixable in a couple of emails, and we'd genuinely rather get the software working for you. Write to helpdesktallybench@gmail.com describing what happened. If we can't fix it, we'll refund you — asking for support first doesn't cost you the refund or restart the 7 days.

How to request a refund

Email helpdesktallybench@gmail.com with:

  • The email address used for the purchase, and the order or payment reference
  • Which edition you bought (Standard or Pro)
  • What went wrong

How long a refund takes

We aim to respond to refund requests within 2 working days. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 working days of approval. How quickly it then appears on your statement depends on your bank or card issuer and is outside our control.

What happens to your licence after a refund

When a purchase is refunded, the associated licence key is revoked and the software will stop being usable on that computer. Please uninstall it. You can export your inventory data to CSV or take a backup before requesting a refund if you want to keep your records.
