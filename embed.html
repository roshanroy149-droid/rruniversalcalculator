How it works

Three steps, about a minute.

1. Copy the code

Find your calculator in the list below and press Copy embed code. You get an <iframe> plus a few lines of script that let the frame resize itself to the calculator's height, so your readers never see an internal scrollbar.

2. Paste it into your page

Anywhere in your HTML. It works in WordPress (use a Custom HTML block), Ghost, Squarespace, Webflow, plain HTML, or any CMS that lets you insert raw HTML. The iframe is capped at 640px wide and scales down on mobile.

3. Keep the credit link

A small "Calculator by TallyBench" line renders under the embedded tool. That link is the whole price of admission — leave it in place and you are free to use the calculator commercially, on as many pages as you like, forever.

Does the embed slow my page down?

The iframe is marked loading="lazy", so browsers defer loading it until the reader scrolls near it. The calculator itself is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no frameworks and no third-party requests, so once it does load it is a small download.

What if I don't want the resize script?

Drop it. The <iframe> alone works perfectly well — it simply stays at the default 640px height instead of shrinking to fit the calculator, which for most tools means some empty space at the bottom.

Can I embed a calculator on a page that also runs ads?

Yes. Commercial use is fine, ads included. We ask only that the credit link stays visible and is not marked nofollow, since that link is what makes this worth offering.

Something is broken or you want a calculator that doesn't exist yet

Both are worth telling us about — the contact route is on the About page. A calculator that someone actually wants to embed is the best possible argument for building it.
